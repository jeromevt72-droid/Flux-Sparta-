QUESTIONS

Before you play.

Is FLUX really free?

Yes. The full game, every run, and the global leaderboard are free with no purchase required. Cosmetic Worlds are optional and never affect gameplay fairness.

Do I need to make an account?

No signup is required to play. Your personal best is saved on your device automatically.

What happens to my score if I switch devices?

Your scores stay on the global leaderboard. Your pilot itself is saved on the device where you play, and Safari and the Home Screen app count as separate places. To keep playing as the same pilot, tap EDIT next to your pilot name in the game, then RESTORE CODE, and save the code. Then enter it on the new device, in the Home Screen app, or after clearing your data.

What devices does FLUX work on?

Any modern phone, tablet, or desktop browser. FLUX also installs as an app on iOS and Android — see the install steps above.

Is the Global Grid country competition live yet?

Yes — the Global Grid shows real, live totals. Each pilot's best score counts toward their country, so every new player and every new personal best moves the board.

Will there be more Worlds?

Yes. Solar Inferno is live now, and more Worlds are in active development.
