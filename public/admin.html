EXCEPTIONS NEEDING ATTENTION

FIND A PLAYER

Remove score takes a player's scores off the public boards. It keeps their FLUX ID and purchases — and they can post new scores. For repeated abuse, use Restrict: they keep playing, but nothing they submit appears publicly. Restrictions follow a player's device identity; if they clear their browser data, they return with a new tag.

ADVANCED

PURCHASE RECORDS

Look up by the buyer's email address (from their Stripe receipt) or a payment reference (pi_…) from the Stripe dashboard. Works even if the buyer never played a ranked run.

PRIVACY DELETION REQUEST

Find the player above, then use Privacy delete on their card. It erases their scores, name and flags. Purchases are kept unless you tick the box. If the player is restricted, the restriction record (tag, date, reason) is kept while it is in force, as the Privacy Policy explains.

NAME BANS

An exact name, shown as PILOT from any device. The player's best is kept.
